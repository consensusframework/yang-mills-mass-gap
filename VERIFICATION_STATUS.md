## The three boxes

Following Kip Thorne's discipline for *Interstellar* — every scientific element
labelled as **established truth**, **educated guess**, or **speculation**, and
never allowed to masquerade as one another — every statement in this repository
falls into exactly one box:

### 📗 Box 1 — Established truth (machine-checked)
Statements proven in Lean 4 with `#print axioms` showing only the standard
foundations (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). Everything in the
active tree (`Phase3/LatticeGauge`) is in this box.

### 📙 Box 2 — Educated guess, 📕 Box 3 — Speculation
These boxes were populated only by the legacy Phases 1 and 2 (`axiom`
declarations matching literature results, and axioms equivalent to open
problems, including the existence of the Yang–Mills measure and the mass gap
itself). **No theorem that consumes a Box-3 axiom constitutes progress on the
Clay problem.** Since the reorganization these boxes are empty in the active
tree; the classification is preserved in `AXIOM_AUDIT.md` of the archived
tree (see below).
